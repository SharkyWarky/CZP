Zombies in Spaceland

Zombies in Spaceland is the first Zombies map featured in Call of Duty: Infinite Warfare, launching with the rest of the game on November 4 for the Playstation 4, Xbox One, and PC. It features a brand new cast of four aspiring actors who audition for a role in eccentric filmmaker Willard Wyler's newest film, the titular Zombies in Spaceland. However, Wyler commits a ritual that sucks the four actors into the film, causing them to be the stars of Zombies in Spaceland.

Overview

Zombies in Spaceland is set entirely in the "Spaceland" amusement park, allowing players to ride rides, play games, and explore the behind the scenes aspects of a theme park all while surviving against the Zombie horde. The map features brand new mechanics such as arcade machines like the Ghosts N Skulls and David Hasselhoff as the park's DJ.

The map contains many classic elements of the Treyarch Zombies mode, although most features have been retooled or at least given a new look to better fit Infinite Warfare's aesthetic. Gameplay basics such as zombies, rounds, points, and difficulty increases as the game goes on are all present. Power-Ups, Perks, and the Pack-a-Punch Machine all return. The Mystery Box is also present, but now known as the Magic Wheel.

Perks are now Candy Perks, and are mostly equivalent or slightly modified from the Perk-a-Cola of previous games. Zombies in Spaceland does introduce a completely new perk, Trail Blazers. Similarly, the selection of Power-Ups is nearly equivalent to past games, with the addition of the Infinite Ammo and Infinite Grenades power-ups.

Buildables also return in Zombies in Spaceland. The buildables in this map include N31L the robot, the Seti-Com, and the four Weapons of Rock Wonder Weapons; the Shredder, the Head Cutter, the Dischord, and the Face Melter.


In addition to the Weapons of Rock, the map contains two more Wonder Weapons. The Forge Freeze, which can be obtained as a prize from the arcade, and the Arcane Core attachment which gives regular projectile weapons Wonder Weapon like powers and effects.

While players do fight standard Zombies, Zombies in Spaceland introduces two other primary enemies; the special round Clown Zombie and the elite Brute.

Being that Zombies in Spaceland is the first map in the Infinite Warfare zombies series, it also introduces many new items and gameplay staples of this iteration of the mode. The Armageddon, the Cryo Grenade, Rewind, Transponder, Portal Generator, Black Hole Grenade, Plasma Grenade, Cluster Grenade, C4, Concussion Grenade, Bio Spike and Gas Grenade throwables are all introduced. The map brings the new Souvenir Coin Machine for you to build traps that can help you deal with the horde. The map also includes the Arcade and the Lost and Found mechanics. Unique to this map, players may find Shades, allowing them to see hidden targets around the map.

The maps Easter Egg directly pits players against a hostile faction of Alien (Infinite Warfare), in order to obtain a piece of the Soul Key and allow the actors to escape the film.

Layout

Zombies in Spaceland is a large map, with more open lanes. The map is split into five different sections:

Entrance/Junction

The players spawn at the entrance of Spaceland, where a power switch can be located at the back room to their left, which powers up the area of the entrance and the Junction area. Players can purchase the Hailstorm or the M1 at the entrance for 500 points per weapon, or buy Up 'N Atoms (500 on solo up to three times, 1500 in co-op).

A barricade stands between the entrance and the Junction area. Here, the players can find a Souvenir Token machine, N31L the Robot, a Fortune Teller, as well as a possible spawn location for a Seti-Com part. The Banshee and Oni are also available for purchase.In the center is the main Portal, where after the other four Portals have been traversed, will open the entrance to the Pack-a-Punch projector machine. While inside the Pack-a-Punch room, players have 30 seconds to upgrade their weapons, activate the Trap UFOs, or complete the crystal cabinet inside in order to build the Exquisite Core. After accessing the room, it will become unavailable for some time. From the Junction, the players can choose to go to one of the three areas by donating and opening barriers to: Journey into Space, Polar peak, or Kepler System.

Journey into Space

Journey into Space, going left from the Junction, is comprised of three separate areas (four if the Astrocade is counted). The first area is immediately beyond its corresponding junction door and contains an Erad wall-buy, a Magic Wheel, a trap, a DJ spawn location, a souvenir station, a path to the Astrocade, a door to the Underground and another door to the second area. Here players can also find the board to place parts for the Face Melter quest buildable weapon. The second area contains a Volk wall-buy, another Magic Wheel, another trap, a Fortune Teller, a Teleport Portal, the Tuff Nuff perk and a door. This door leads to the third area which contains a continually-active trap in the form of Dodgems carts (which are permanently active or disabled depending on whether the power is on or not), the Blue Bolts perk, a Karma-45 wall-buy, a second entrance to the Astrocade and another entrance to the Underground. Between the Dodgems cart trap and the Astrocade entrance is another Magic Wheel.

Polar Peaks

Polar Peaks is straight ahead from the Junction. Up the long tunnel players will first see an RPR EVO wall buy and a trap. To the right is a portal, a door which to the left are the spaceship fountains or down straight to a door to the underground. Here players can also find the board to place parts for the Head Cutter quest buildable weapon. Up the stairs either direction are two Magic Wheels, an HVR and Volk wall buy, Quickies, a door down towards the Astrocade, and the Triton roller coaster game ride.

Kepler System

To the right of the Junction is the Kepler System part of the park. To the left past the barricade is a souvenir machine, a portal, and up the left stairs is a Magic Wheel and another barricade going down towards Triton Mountain or the spaceship fountains. To the right is the Octonian Hunter shooting game. A permanent trap is in the form of a tick-tocking crocodile mouth which crushes anything under its jaws after 3 seconds of a player stepping in its mouth, which can also down a player even with Tuff Nuffs. Past that are an Erad and HVR wall buy, a trap, a Fortune Teller, a Magic Wheel, Bomb Stoppers, and Slappy Taffy.

Spaceship Fountains

The Spaceship Fountains is a circle of water spouts that fire upward once the power is turned on, shooting the player up into the air, and killing Zombies that get caught in it. The only way to get here is by a path either from Polar Peaks or an Underground door and over a tiny bridge past a DJ location. Here players can buy Bang Bangs. One of the fountains is tilted and shoots at an angle, and when standing on it, will catapult the player over into the Kepler System. Past this area is another small bridge that leads to a Magic Wheel, and next to that is a slide that goes back down to the Junction area and next to the Polar Peak entrance.

Underground

The Underground has three entrances and acts as an underground tunnel system between the Astrocade, Journey into Space and Kepler System. The Kepler system entrance is located between Polar Peak and Kepler System, while the other two entrances are both located near Journey into Space and the Astrocade. The first Underground entrance located between Journey into Space and Astrocade is located just beyond the path to the Astrocade at the main junction door and this entrance leads to approximately halfway between Kepler System and the other entrance in the Underground. The Underground contains a Banshee wall-buy and an HVR wall-buy, as well as the Racin' Stripes perk. Here players can also find the board to place parts for the Shredder quest buildable weapon.

Astrocade

The Astrocade has three entrances (one that leads to Journey into Space, another that leads to Polar Peak and a third that leads to the main junction) and contains the Disco trap, multiple arcade machines (some of which are playable and reward tickets), a Teleport Portal and an NV4 wall-buy. In the back corner is a prize booth where players can exchange tickets for ammo refill, grenade refill, Cryo grenades, or the Forge Freeze. The Golden Teeth can be purchased for 300 tickets in order to open the cabinet inside the crocodile trap in the Kepler System. Here players can also find the board to place parts for the Dischord quest buildable weapon

Triton Rollercoaster

Players can ride the roller coaster that also serves as a shooting game. Throughout the ride, players have to shoot targets to earn tickets, disable lasers and open doors. Zombie Clowns will also appear on the tracks, and players must shoot them or else take lots of explosive damage and get downed in the middle of the ride.
