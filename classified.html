Map Index

Classified is a Zombies map that is included with the Black Ops Pass for Call of Duty: Black Ops 4. The fourth map featured in the game and the twenty-eighth map overall, it is a reimagining of the map "Five", which was previously featured in Call of Duty: Black Ops.
The map features the "Ultimis" crew - the original versions of"Tank" Dempsey, Nikolai Belinksi, Takeo Masaki and Edward Richtofen - as the four playable characters, replacing John F. Kennedy, Robert McNamara, Richard Nixon, and Fidel Castro. The map's story follows them as they investigate The Pentagon after being teleported there from Shangri-La via overloading a teleporter with the 31-79 JGb215
With the release of Operation Grand Heist, Classified can now be purchased as a standalone map without having to acquire the Black Ops Pass for $7.99 USD

Overview

Being a reimagining of "Five", Classified's layout is mostly identical. However, like Blood of the Dead, the remake of Mob of the Dead, there are a few new rooms added to the map, including the Central Filing and Main Offices on the first floor, and the Server Room in the War Room. The Perk-a-Colas are replaced by the new perk system introduced in Black Ops 4. In addition to this, the steps taken to reach the Pack-a-Punch Machine have also changed. There are also several new Mystery Box locations as well, with the initial Mystery Box location being either in the Main Offices or on the upper level of the War Room.

Story

In Classified's Intro Cutscene, the Ultimis crew is shown attempting to teleport from Shangri-La to Groom Lake in order to continue Richtofen's Grand Scheme. Just as they are about to leave, Tank fires the 31-79 JGb215, accidentally overloading the teleporter and sending them hurtling through space and time to the Pentagon in 1963. Ultimis, finding the Pentagon mostly abandoned, begin to loot the facility of its weapons and supplies, including the Winter's Howl. Samantha Maxis, still in control of the Zombies and trapped in the M.P.D on the moon, witnesses these events Simultaneously, Anton Gersh and Yuri Zavoyski activate a Gersh Device for the first time trapping Gersch inside the Casimir Mechanism and allowing Samantha to corrupt Yuri and send him to the Pentagon. Upon arrival, Yuri initiates a Zombie outbreak but is prevented from participating himself by Samantha. The Zombies attack Ultimis Ultimis endeavors to leave the Pentagon and arrive at Groom Lake, building the Teleporter Signal Amplifier in order to do so. Upon arriving, however, they discover a mostly abandoned Groom Lake with no known connection to the Moon. Samantha also speaks directly to Richtofen, discouraging him from continuing his quest and insuring him she will do everything in her power to make him fail Through unknown means, Ultimis is able to re-link the Groom Lake teleporter with Griffin Station, and the events of Moon play out as they originally did. Some time after, Ultimis returns to Earth and travels to some point in time before it was destroyed. They are captured and imprisoned by the remains by American forces, but are rescued by "Primis". "Primis" Nikolai recruits them to fight in the Great War, and the two groups depart through a portal

Gameplay

As could be expected, the map plays much like "Five". The map is split into three floors, the Office Floor, War Room, and Laboratories. Initially, the player can only travel between levels using Elevators, which cost 500 points to use. After turning on the power, teleporters activate on all floors, allowing the map to be more quickly and easily traversed. The location the player will teleport to is random, but the player can at least control what level they go to by paying attention to the symbol floating in the teleporter. An Office Chair brings the player to the Offices, A Control Panel brings the player to the War Room, and A Flask brings the player to one of the teleporters in the labs Once the power is on, 4 "DEFCON" switches can be flipped in the War Room to lower the Pentagons security and allow the player to teleport to new areas. Initially, activating DEFCON 5 will bring the player to the "Panic Room". The Panic room includes a piece of the Teleporter Signal Amplifier, and is initially free of Zombies for 30 seconds, providing players with a slight break. Once the Teleporter Signal Amplifier is built and in place, the War Room's central teleporter will bring players to Groom Lake, where they can use the Pack-a-Punch Machine The map also introduces two new buildables; the Riot Shield and the Teleporter Signal Amplifier, the latter being used to access the Pack-a-Punch Machine. With this modified teleporter, players are able to use the Pack-a-Punch Machine found in Groom Lake, the same place featured in the map Moon. The Electro-Shock Defenses return as well, although the locations for the parts needed to repair them are in different locations, with one spawning in the Server Room and the other spawning in the Porcine Research lab. The Winter's Howl also returns, and is obtainable from the Mystery Box and through completing the Project Skadi quest Unlike the original version, the Pentagon Thief is absent; he is instead replaced by Hellhounds. Like the original map, the Crawler Zombies return, and can now spawn on other levels of the map instead of just the Laboratories. The Bonfire Sale power-up also returns, and can be acquired by opening up areas of Groom Lake, which is achieved by surviving in said area every few rounds without leaving or going down Unlike other maps in Black Ops 4, Classified does not have a main quest. Instead, the ending cutscene plays automatically once players complete round 150
