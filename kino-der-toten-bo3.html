Samantha's Sorrow

Rewards: max ammo

  1. Head to the outside courtyard and stand next to the blue metal door
  2. You will hear a sequence of 3 codes comprised of 3 knocks. Once the sequence has finished, knife the door in the same pattern
    Possible Codes:
    • 1-1-5
    • 1-2-1
    • 1-4-3
    • 2-4-1
    • 3-2-1
    • 5-1-2
    • 5-3-4
    • 6-2-4
    • 6-6-6
    • 9-3-5
  3. Head to the main room and interact with the samantha doll infront of the projector lying on the ground to the right of the stage
  4. You must now find 5 samantha dolls within the theater and shoot them (must do within a time limit. You will hear a laugh if you failed and you must restart from the previous step)
    • On a seat on your right, just before turning in the Juggernog's machine corner
    • Outside the playable area, on the right of Juggernog's machine
    • On the window of the projection room, above the theater
    • Pending down the chandelier in the main theater
    • On a chair, on the first line of seats, on your right when facing the stage
    • Down on the ground on the corner of the room, on the left of the stage
    • On a balcoony up on the right of the theater when facing the stage
    • On a metallic platform, up on the right corner of the stage
    • Behind a fake wall, on the stage ontop of the beam
  5. After shooting all the dolls, return to the doll you first activated to recieve a max ammo and play the song

115

Interact with 3 element 115 rocks around the map

  1. Spawn Room: On a stand or corner on the right side as you enter from the starting area
  2. Dressing Room / Mannequin Room: On a table or corner behind the mannequins on the right side of the stage
  3. Storage / Film Room: On the lower shelf of a rack in the back storage room past the alley

Pack-A-Punch

  1. Turn on Power
  2. Enter the teleporter on the stage and activate it
  3. head to the starting room and interact with the platform on the floor to link the teleporter
  4. stand in the teleporter to be teleporter to PaP

Movie Projector

Compeleting this EE will paly a movie on the projector screen in the main room

  • Locate 3 reels located in the rooms you teleport to after using the PaP Teleporter (can only grab one at a time)
    • Samantha's Room
      • On the Table
      • On the Bed
      • Leaning against the rocking chair
    • Samatha's Destroyed Room
      • On the bed
      • Leaning on the overturned table
      • near the window
    • Dentist's Office
      • On the cart next to the chair
      • On one of the 2 counters
    • Conference Room
      • On the TV
      • Under the projector
      • on the table infront of you
  • After Grabbing one reel, you must return it to the projector in PaP before you can grab another
