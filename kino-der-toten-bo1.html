115

Interact with 3 element 115 rocks around the map

  1. Spawn Room: On a stand or corner on the right side as you enter from the starting area
  2. Dressing Room / Mannequin Room: On a table or corner behind the mannequins on the right side of the stage
  3. Storage / Film Room: On the lower shelf of a rack in the back storage room past the alley

Thundergun

Found in Mystery Box

Ray Gun

Found in Mystery Box

Power

Switch is located in the backstage area

Pack-A-Punch

  1. Turn on Power
  2. Enter the teleporter on the stage and activate it
  3. head to the starting room and interact with the platform on the floor to link the teleporter
  4. stand in the teleporter to be teleporter to PaP

Movie Projector

Compeleting this EE will paly a movie on the projector screen in the main room

  • Locate 3 reels located in the rooms you teleport to after using the PaP Teleporter (can only grab one at a time)
    • Samantha's Room
      • On the Table
      • On the Bed
      • Leaning against the rocking chair
    • Samatha's Destroyed Room
      • On the bed
      • Leaning on the overturned table
      • near the window
    • Surgery Room
      • On the cart next to the chair
      • On one of the 2 counters
    • Conference Room
      • On the TV
      • Under the projector
      • on the table infront of you
  • After Grabbing one reel, you must return it to the projector in PaP before you can grab another

Quick Revive

Located in the starting spawn room, on the right side next to the bar/barrier

Juggernog

Located in the main theater area, near the seating rows on the left side when entering from spawn (requires power)

Double Tap Root Beer

Located outside in the back alleyway area past the fire trap room

Speed Cola

Located in the large dressing/storage room downstairs past the double stairs from the foyer

Paintings

Interacting with each of the portaits on the wall results in different dialogue from your character

Rocket Ship

Does Nothing

  1. Turn on the Power
  2. Collect all 3 film reels
  3. Shoot 2 radios found around the map (audio will play when hit)
    1. One on the chandelier in the main room
    2. in the alleyway near double tap outside the fence barrier. below the fence of the guard tower.
  4. Use the Teleporter to enter the pentagon's office
  5. Interact with the rocket figurine to launch it
