Shangri-La Overview

Shangri-La is a Zombies map featured in Call of Duty: Black Ops III as part of the Zombies Chronicles DLC.

Game

Call of Duty: Black Ops III

Original Release

Call of Duty: Black Ops (2011)

Location

A lost jungle temple hidden in the Himalayas.

Playable Characters

  • Tank Dempsey
  • Nikolai Belinski
  • Takeo Masaki
  • Edward Richtofen

Time Travel Will Tell - Main Easter Egg

Requirements

  • Activate Pack-a-Punch
  • Complete the eclipse sequence
  • Collect the focusing stone

Steps

  1. Activate the eclipse by using the switches around the map.
  2. Complete the temple puzzle.
  3. Use the shrink ray on required objects.
  4. Collect the focusing stone.

Reward

Completion of the Easter Egg awards the achievement/trophy and progresses the Zombies storyline.

Music Easter Egg

Shangri-La's music Easter Egg is activated by interacting with three meteorite fragments hidden around the map.

Locations

  1. Starting area near the Quick Revive area.
  2. Near the water slide area.
  3. Inside the temple area.

Song

"Pareidolia" - Elena Siegman

31-79 JGb215 Shrink Ray

The 31-79 JGb215 is Shangri-La's unique Wonder Weapon. It shrinks zombies into miniature versions that can be crushed by melee attacks.

Pack-a-Punch Upgrade

The Pack-a-Punched version is called the "31-79 JGb215 Mk II".

Side Easter Eggs

  • Focusing stone quest
  • Eclipse activation
  • Monkey bomb interactions
  • Radio transmissions

Buildables

Shangri-La does not contain traditional buildable equipment.

Perks

  • Quick Revive
  • Jugger-Nog
  • Speed Cola
  • Double Tap Root Beer
  • PhD Flopper (BO3 custom availability varies)

Weapons

Wall Weapons

  • MP40
  • AK-74u
  • M16

Wonder Weapons

  • 31-79 JGb215 Shrink Ray
