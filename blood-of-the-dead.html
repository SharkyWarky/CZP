Blood of the Dead

Blood of the Dead is the third Zombies map that is featured in Call of Duty: Black Ops 4, and the twenty-seventh map overall. It is a reimagined version of, as well as a sequel to, the fan-favorite map Mob of the Dead from Call of Duty: Black Ops II.

Overview

Blood of the Dead, as a follow-up to Mob of the Dead, retains most of the original maps locations and gameplay features while including multiple additions and twists of its own

Blood of the Dead includes most of the original areas from Mob of the Dead, with the only substantial cut being the Golden Gate Bridge. Certain areas have also been modified, such as only half of the roof being accessible, a new entrance to the Mob of the Dead starting room being added, and a few of the locations for aircraft parts on Mob of the Dead are either inaccessible or heavily changed. More notably, Blood of the Dead includes a number of brand new areas such as Richtofen's lab, New Industries, the Power House, the Warden's House, and the dangerous Catwalk and Recreation Yard respectively. As a result of these changes, most gameplay elements like the Pack-a-Punch Machine, Perk Machines, Cerberus, and buildable parts have had their locations altered

Blood of the Dead features the Primis crew - "Tank" Dempsey, Nikolai Belinksi, Takeo Masaki, and Edward Richtofen - as the four playable characters, replacing Albert "Weasel" Arlington, Finn O'Leary, Salvatore "Sal" DeLuca, and Billy Handsome. Brutus also makes a return, this time as the main antagonist of the map, as the Warden. Hellhounds are also present on the map, originally only being available in the Grief version of Mob of the Dead, Cell Block

Gameplay wise a number of items and equipment from Mob of the Dead return and have been expanded on. The Blundergat and it's Acidgat upgrade are both present, as well as the brand new Magmagat. The Hell's Retriever, as well as it's Hell's Redeemer upgrade, returns following a short quest. The Golden Spork can be obtained, with two new upgrades available, the Golden Scalpel and the Golden Spork Knife. The Acid Trap and Saw Trap both return, though the Tower Trap is collapsed and unusable. The most notable change is that Afterlife is no longer a gameplay mechanic, though elements of it have been wrapped into the new Spectral Shield. Specifically, the Spectral Shield allows players to still power the Voltmeters around the map, to access various utilities and progress quests

Blood of the Deads plot finds the Primis crew trapped in the Alcatraz Pocket Dimension, following Albert Arlington breaking the cycle of the multiverse. As a result, they must find a way to escape in the new main quest, Most Escape Alive

Hell's Redeemer - Video

  1. Obtain the Hell's Retriever and Spectral Shield
  2. Navigate to the Recreation Yard and stand near to the flaming barrel. Kill Zombies using the Hell's Retriever, if the player is in the correct area of the Recreation Yard the Hell's Retriever will glow blue when being thrown. Once you have killed enough zombies with the blue tomahawk a howl can be heard and the Hell's Retriever will no longer glow blue
  3. Locate a blue hellhound mark around the map using the Spectral Shield vision. Throw the Hell's Retriever at the hellhound mark, if done correctly the mark will disappear and the Hell's Retriever will be taken out of the player's inventory. Locations
  4. Progress until a Hellhound only round. Locate a ghost dog walking anywhere around the map as indicated by its paw prints, the dog is entirely visible when using the Spectral Shield's vision. Once the player is close to the ghost dog, Spirit Blast the dog using the Spectral Shield
  5. Take the fast travel from the Warden's House to the Showers (or vice versa). During this travel the Hell's Redeemer is located on a rock, obtain it by holding the activate button once the player is nearing it

Silver Spoon - Video

  1. Obtain the Spectral Shield
  2. Navigate to the Warden‘s Office. Using the Spectral Shield's vision, locate and note the 3 numbers on the pillars
  3. Navigate to the number pad in the Citadel Tunnels, activate the pad by Spirit Blasting with the Spectral Shield. Enter the previously-obtained numbers. If done correctly, the elevator will drop
  4. Navigate to the voltmeter at the Docks, Spirit Blast it to activate the crane. Once the crane has positioned itself over the Docks, throw a Hell's Retriever at the skeleton within the net, cutting off its arm
  5. The Spoon will fall and be available to equip on the crate below the crane

Golden Spork - Video

  1. Navigate to the bathtub located in the Infirmary. Next, place the Spoon in the tub by interacting with it
  2. Navigate to the Roof, begin to kill Zombies using the Acidgat or Magmagat. Blood will begin to drip in the tub from the previous step
  3. Once the tub has filled all the way, activate it to drain it
  4. Locate the water tower near the Catwalk, if all has been done correctly the water tower will be leaking blood. Shoot all of the yellow supports off the Water Tower in order to make the Tower fall down. Once the Water Tower collapses, throw the Hell's Retriever at a branch in front of the tower to collect and equip the Golden Spork

Golden Scalpel - Video

  1. Navigate near the tub of blood in New Industries and get ~100 melee kills nearby until the tub changes to a dark red colour. Once the colour changes, interact with the tub to place the golden spoon in the tub
  2. Next you will need to throw the Hell's Redeemer at 3 coloured rocks
    • Green - D-Block / MOTD original spawn - outside the barricade
    • Blue - Docks / Shield Blast the Electric Box - inside the net
    • Red - Eagle Plaza / Next to the Warden's House - on the corner of this roof
  3. Once all 3 have been collected place them at their corresponding traps and get 100 kills using the traps
    • Green - Acid Trap - Cafeteria
    • Blue - Fan Trap - Warden's Office
    • Red - Spin Trap - New Industries
  4. Once you've got 100 kills at each location the stones will become shiny gems and you can interact with them to pick them up
  5. Use the Cafeteria Fast Travel towards New Industries and as soon as you arrive, turn around and throw your Hell's Redeemer here. If done correctly, a golden nugget should be left on the floor behind you. Pick it up and take it to the forge to create the Golden Scalpel

Golden Spork-Knife - Video

  1. Head to the New Industries and interact with the blood tub for the skeleton hand to take the Golden Scalpel
  2. Take a Brutus (you can use code 666 to get one earlier, can only be used once per game) onto the Catwalk and melee kill him infront of the sign with a knife in it (can use dying wish + etherial razer to make him die faster). Step is done correctly when the prison number under the knfe on the sign has fallen.
  3. Return to the tub and interact with it to recieve the Golden Spork-Knife
